1The payoff moment, and the record it leads to

Three takes on “you’re connected” and three on the meeting record, each carrying a smaller version of the ring diagram so the person you just met arrives with their own shape. One constraint worth stating plainly, because it changes the copy: there is no reconnect action in the backend — removing a connection is one-way, and getting it back means meeting in person again. So the button after a removal can’t restore anything; what it can honestly do is take you to Connect, which is what 1g does.
